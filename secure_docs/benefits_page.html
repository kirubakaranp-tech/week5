Employee Benefits at Northwind

Northwind Software looks after its people. This page lists the main benefits for full-time employees in India.

Health Insurance

Every employee is covered by group health insurance of Rs 5 lakh per year.

The cover includes your spouse, two children and your parents at no extra cost.

Learning Budget

Each employee can spend up to Rs 40,000 a year on courses, books and certifications.

  • Online courses and certification exams
  • Technical books and e-books
  • One conference ticket per year

Work From Home

Employees can work from home on up to 2 days a week and get Rs 1,000 a month for internet.

Wellness

Free yoga classes run every Wednesday in the Bengaluru and Chennai offices.
